# Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Recovery is a private personal-use application.

## Data Access

The application may access Google Drive in order to create, read, update, and restore encrypted backup data belonging to the application owner.

## Data Storage

Backup contents are encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive.

The application does not intentionally transmit Google Drive data to third-party services for advertising, analytics, or resale.

## Data Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## Users

This application is intended only for use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

## Contact

For questions about this application or this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the Google account associated with the OAuth application.
